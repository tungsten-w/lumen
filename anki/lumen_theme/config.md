Lumen paints Anki with the palette noctalia extracts from your wallpaper.
Changes here apply the next time a page is drawn; colours themselves update live.

- `wallpaper` — show the current Lumen wallpaper behind the main window.
- `wallpaper_blur` — blur strength for it, `0` for a sharp image.
- `wallpaper_dim` — how much of the palette's surface colour covers it, `0` to `1`.
- `follow_lumen_mode` — switch Anki between light and dark along with Lumen.
- `restyle_cards` — draw cards on a glass panel, with the palette and `card_font`.
  Turn it off if your note types carry their own design.
- `font`, `card_font` — font families for the interface and for cards.
- `animations` — rise-in, glow and gradient motion. This decides on its own: Anki's *Reduce motion* only covers Anki's own transitions.
- `qt_theme` — recolour Anki's native windows and menus as well.
- `poll_ms` — how often to look for a new palette or wallpaper.
